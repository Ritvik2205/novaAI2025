## Engagement Lifecycle

1. **Intake & Scoping**
   - Lead concierge agent captures goals, constraints, required deliverables, and success criteria.
   - Operations coordinator validates legal requirements (NDA, security posture, procurement path).
   - Discovery packet is generated automatically in the CRM and shared with the partner for confirmation.

2. **Team Assembly**
   - Student groups are ranked by domain expertise, availability, and past performance.
   - Primary pod is booked for the core delivery. Shadow apprentices are attached when the project can support additional learners.
   - Kick-off checklist ensures tooling, calendar, and communications are configured before work begins.

3. **Execution Rhythm**
   - Weekly sprint reviews led by the student product manager; mentor and partner representatives attend.
   - Agents log meeting notes, action items, and risk alerts directly into the CRM.
   - Quality gates: architecture review for technical builds, user testing milestone for experience design, and compliance review for data-heavy work.

4. **Delivery & Support**
   - Final delivery bundle includes code, documentation, demo assets, and an operations hand-off guide.
   - 30-day shadow support window where the pod responds to clarifications or bug fixes.
   - Transition plan recommends maintenance options: continue with ScottyLabs retainer, hand-off to partner engineering, or connect with alumni freelancers.
