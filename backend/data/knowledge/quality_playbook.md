#### Quality & Compliance Playbook

- **Design Reviews:** All solution sketches must be critiqued by a mentor or industry advisor before build.
- **Security Baseline:** Agentic software engagements must pass static analysis, dependency scanning, and prompt-injection resilience testing. Hardware/fabrication projects follow OSHA and CMU lab safety guidelines.
- **Documentation:** Deliverables include README with deployment steps, decision log, and maintenance checklist. For physical builds, include materials list, structural calculations, and inspection photos.
- **Data Governance:** Any dataset shared by partners is tagged with handling instructions. Sensitive data remains in partner-controlled storage with temporary signed access for pods.
- **Feedback Loop:** Partners submit sprint feedback via embedded forms; results feed the agent’s tone calibration and highlight coaching opportunities.

Incident workflow:

1. Agent raises a red flag when acceptance tests fail twice or a partner reports a critical issue.
2. Operations lead assigns a fix owner within four hours and schedules a response call.
3. Post-mortem is documented in the CRM, including root cause, remediation, and prevention actions.
