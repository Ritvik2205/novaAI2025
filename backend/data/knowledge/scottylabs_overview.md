ScottyLabs is Carnegie Mellon's student-run guild for agentic product development. Our mission is to pair multidisciplinary student builders with companies who need rapid experimentation, prototyping, or operational automation.

Key pillars:

- Studio pods: cross-functional teams of 4–6 students staffed with a product manager, technical lead, automation engineer, and design specialist.
- Faculty mentors: every engagement has a CMU faculty advisor who conducts weekly design reviews and ensures academic compliance.
- Layered agent stack: ScottyLabs uses OpenRouter-powered service agents for discovery, quoting, and execution oversight. All agents sync customer context back into Agentuity so humans always have the latest state.

Standard outcomes:

1. Discovery brief — compiled within three business days, summarising goals, constraints, and success metrics.
2. Sprint zero prototype — functional demo or architectural plan delivered by the end of week two.
3. Delivery retrospective — session summarising delivered artefacts, recommended follow-on work, and ops hand-off notes.

Success is measured using partner satisfaction (NPS ≥ 8), on-time delivery (≥ 95%), and student skill development tracked via post-project reflections.
