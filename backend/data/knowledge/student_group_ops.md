### Student Group Operating Model

- **Domains:** We maintain specialised guilds in applied AI, automation, product design, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and experiential fabrication (treehouses, installations, adaptive furniture).
- **Availability Matrix:** Pods publish weekly capacity blocks. The CRM syncs this matrix with the scheduling agent to prevent double booking.
- **Mentorship:** Every pod is paired with an alumni advisor. Advisors run biweekly retros, approve scope changes, and coordinate with the partner when escalations arise.
- **Quality Metrics:** Each engagement is scored across delivery quality, communication, and partner satisfaction. Pods need a rolling average ≥ 4.5/5 to remain client-facing.
- **Libraries & Templates:** Reusable components live in the SuperNOVA registry: prompt templates, automation blueprints, billing calculators, and compliance checklists.

Escalations:

1. Delivery blocker detected → agent notifies operations lead → stand-up war-room with partner sponsor.
2. Scope change request → pod lead drafts impact statement → mentor reviews → partner approves digitally from the CRM portal.
3. Contractor replacement → bench pod is suggested by the recommendation engine → operations lead executes transition plan within 24 hours.
